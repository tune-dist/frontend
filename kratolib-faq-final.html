03 — Royalties & earnings

Getting paid

Rough per-stream royalty estimates for India:

Spotify — ₹0.25–₹0.40 per stream
Apple Music — ₹0.50–₹0.80 per stream (higher payout)
JioSaavn — ₹0.05–₹0.15 per stream
YouTube Music — varies with ad revenue
CRBT / Caller Tune — ₹1–₹5 per caller tune set (significant India income)
On all Kratolib paid plans you keep 100% of your royalties — every rupee earned goes directly to you. A traditional label deal would typically take 50–80% of these same earnings.
Royalties are collected from all platforms and credited to your Kratolib wallet monthly. Platforms typically report earnings 45–60 days after the streaming month ends.

On Growth Label and above: on-demand payouts — withdraw your earnings any time without waiting for the standard cycle.

Payout methods: NEFT/IMPS bank transfer and UPI — all in INR, no forex conversion required.
If you collaborated on a track — with a producer, co-writer, or featured artist — a royalty split automatically divides your streaming income at source before payout, based on agreed percentages.

Example: You and your producer agreed on 60/40 — Kratolib distributes it automatically every payout cycle. No manual transfers, no disputes.

We also provide split sheet agreements — legal documentation of the agreed percentages for each collaborator. Available from Growth Label.
SoundExchange is a US royalty collection body that collects neighboring rights royalties — money earned when your recorded music plays on US digital radio platforms like Pandora, SiriusXM, and iHeartRadio.

Most Indian artists miss this income entirely because they're not registered. Even a moderately popular track can generate thousands of rupees annually from US digital radio plays alone.

Kratolib handles your SoundExchange registration automatically from Growth Label onwards — no extra paperwork, no extra fees.

CRBT (Caller Ring Back Tone) — also known as Jio Hello Tune or Airtel Caller Tune — is the music a caller hears before you pick up. It is one of the most significant revenue streams unique to India.

Kratolib supports CRBT on every plan including the free plan — across Jio, Airtel, Vi, and BSNL. Every time someone sets your song as their caller tune, you earn a royalty.

Most international distribution platforms do not offer this feature at all. For many Indian artists, CRBT income rivals or exceeds Spotify streaming income.
YouTube Content ID automatically detects when someone uses your music in any YouTube video. When your song is detected, you can choose to monetize that video (earn the ad revenue) or block it entirely.

Without Content ID, anyone can use your music on YouTube and you earn nothing. With it, you earn passively from every video that uses your track — even viral ones with millions of views.

Kratolib includes YouTube Content ID on all plans including free. It activates automatically within a few days of your release going live.
Kratolib distributes your lyrics to Musixmatch and Genius from the Solo Pro plan onwards. Once processed:

People who search for your lyrics become new listeners — this is one of the most underrated organic discovery tools for artists.

05 — Marketing & artist tools

Growing your audience

Playlist pitching is submitting your upcoming release directly to Spotify's editorial team for playlist consideration. Your release must be scheduled at least 3 weeks in advance to be eligible.

Kratolib's Solo Pro and above includes pitching support — we guide you on writing effective pitch notes, choosing mood tags, genre targeting, and release timing to maximize your chances of being accepted.

Getting on a mid-size Spotify editorial playlist (50K+ followers) can mean thousands of new streams overnight and trigger Spotify's algorithm to push your track further to more listeners.
A pre-save link lets fans save your unreleased song to their Spotify or Apple Music library before it drops. On release day, it automatically appears in their library — giving you an instant spike in day-one saves and streams.

Spotify's algorithm reads day-one save rates as a quality signal and boosts the track to more listeners. Artists running pre-save campaigns consistently see 2–5x more first-week streams compared to releases with no pre-save promotion.

Available as part of Fan Links & Smart Links on the Growth Label plan and above.
Spotify Discovery Mode lets you signal to Spotify's algorithm that you want a specific track promoted in Radio and Autoplay. In exchange, Spotify takes a slightly lower royalty rate on streams generated through that discovery boost.

Best used for catalog tracks you want to revive, or new releases you want to push hard in the first 4–6 weeks. Think of it as organic algorithmic advertising — a small royalty trade for broader promotion. Available on Solo Pro and above.
